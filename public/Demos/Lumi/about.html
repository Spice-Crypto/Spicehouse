About

Beauty should feel good before you even leave the chair.

Lumi began as a simple idea: a modern Lagos beauty studio built around thoughtful service, polished results and a relaxed experience that feels easy from the very first click.

01 — CONSIDERED

Every service starts with understanding what you actually want.

We ask the right questions, listen to how you want to feel and then shape the result around that.

02 — POLISHED

The goal is a finish that looks intentional without feeling overdone.

We believe beauty works best when it feels elevated, confident and naturally like you.

03 — EASY

From browsing services to booking through WhatsApp, the process should feel simple.

Whether it is a quick silk press or a full wedding-day beauty plan, the experience should stay smooth and calm.

Studio experience

Thoughtful beauty, in a way that feels easy.

Lumi offers a warm, editorial approach to beauty: polished without pressure, detailed without complexity and modern enough for everyday life in Lagos.

Clients come for hair, makeup, bridal looks or a little reset — and leave with something that feels clean, intentional and confidently finished.
