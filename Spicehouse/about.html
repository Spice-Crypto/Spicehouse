About

An independent creative web studio with bigger ambitions.

What we do

We help businesses with strong products, services and identities translate that strength into a stronger online presence.

SpiceHouse is built for businesses that are ready to move beyond DM inboxes and scattered social pages. The aim is simple: give the business a more convincing, better-functioning home online.

We keep the work personal, creative and direct. There is no big agency theatre. Just sharp thinking, thoughtful design and websites that help businesses feel like themselves online.
